About the Game

Faith-Centered Fun
for Every Generation

Revival brings families, youth groups, and small groups together around the Word. Rooted in the Great Commission, this fast-paced card game challenges players to guess words using one-word clues — all while staying on mission and avoiding the Ruin Card.

More than just a game, Revival sparks meaningful conversations about faith, community, and Scripture. Perfect for church events, family game nights, and everything in between.

Our Story

Where Revival Began

This game was created to bring people together around the table — through conversation, laughter, Scripture, and reflection. Revival is not about having all the answers or knowing the Bible well. It is simply an invitation to slow down, connect with one another, and engage with God's Word in a joyful and meaningful way.

The idea for Revival began during a gathering of friends at a YWAM fundraiser to help send a young person into ministry. As a simple word game was played together, laughter filled the room and something special happened — people connected in a way that felt natural and genuine.

That evening sparked a question:

What if a game could create moments like this more often?

Revival was created with that hope in mind — that around tables everywhere, people would laugh together, encourage one another, and experience meaningful conversations about faith.

It is also our hope that Revival can help support future ministry and outreach through a developing mission fund dedicated to encouraging and equipping young people called into service.

Thank you for gathering, playing, and being part of the Revival story.

Gameplay

How to Play

Simple to learn, impossible to put down.

01

Form Your Teams

Split into 2 teams and choose 2 Messengers — one per team — who will give the clues.

02

Lay Out the Grid

Arrange 25 Word Cards face-up in a 5×5 grid at the center of the table.

03

Study the Map

Each Messenger studies the secret Map Card to identify which words belong to their team.

04

Give Clues

Messengers give one-word clues to guide their team toward the right cards.

05

Complete the Mission

Guess all your team's words first — but beware the Ruin Card, which ends the game instantly!
