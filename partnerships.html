Our Mission

Why Revival Exists

At Revival, our mission is to create spaces where people can gather, connect, and explore God's Word together.

We believe meaningful conversation opens hearts, genuine connection builds community, and curiosity invites deeper understanding.

✦

Meaningful Conversation

Every round opens the door to deeper dialogue about faith, life, and Scripture.

✦

Genuine Connection

Around the table, people laugh, encourage one another, and build lasting community.

✦

Curious Faith

Revival meets people where they are — no Bible expertise required, just an open heart.

Looking Ahead

The Revival Missions Fund

From the very beginning, Revival was created with a heart for mission. Our hope is to one day establish a Revival Missions Fund, where a portion of net proceeds from every game sold will go toward supporting youth missions and local leaders who are faithfully walking in obedience to Jesus' call to make disciples of all nations.

Follow us and stay updated as we continue to share stories of how your support is helping impact local communities and the world for the Great Commission.
